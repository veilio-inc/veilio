## Forks and redistributions
You are encouraged to fork and self-host. If you distribute a **modified** build, please give
it **your own name** and make clear it is not the official Veilio. You may note, factually,
that it is "based on Veilio".

## Hosted/commercial use
Running the Community Edition for your own purposes — personal, educational, or commercial — is
fine. **Reselling** it, or offering a **hosted/managed Veilio service to third parties under the
Veilio name**, requires a commercial license and/or written permission — the official hosted
service is **Veilio Cloud**.

To request permission, or if you're unsure whether a use is allowed, contact

brand misuse, not to restrict legitimate discussion or compatible products.
